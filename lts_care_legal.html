Medical Disclaimer
Last updated: June 2026 · Version 1.0

Not Medical Advice

The information provided by LTS Care, including but not limited to health tracking data, trend charts, threshold alerts, correlation displays, and any other outputs, does not constitute medical advice. It is provided for informational and personal tracking purposes only.

Always seek the advice of your physician, transplant team, pulmonologist, or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding your medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you have read or seen in this App.

Not a Diagnostic Tool

LTS Care does not diagnose any medical condition. Alerts, flags, and color-coded indicators within the App are based on general reference ranges and user-defined thresholds for personal awareness only. They are not clinical diagnoses. An alert in this App does not mean you have a medical problem — and the absence of an alert does not mean you are medically clear.

Accuracy of Data

The App relies entirely on data entered by the user. The accuracy of any trend, chart, correlation, or alert is therefore entirely dependent on the accuracy and consistency of user-entered data. The App:

  • Cannot verify the accuracy of data you enter
  • Cannot calibrate or validate the accuracy of home monitoring devices you use
  • May display misleading trends if data is entered inconsistently or incorrectly
  • Should not be used as a substitute for laboratory testing, clinical examination, or professional measurement

Medication Information

Any medication-related features in this App — including medication lists, dose tracking, and correlation displays — are for personal record-keeping only. Never adjust, start, stop, or change any medication based on information displayed in this App without first consulting your healthcare provider. Immunosuppressant medications in particular require precise clinical management and should never be self-adjusted.

Lab Results

Lab result values entered or extracted in this App are for personal tracking and trend awareness only. Clinical interpretation of laboratory values requires a qualified healthcare provider with full knowledge of your medical history and current clinical context. Do not make medical decisions based solely on lab values displayed in this App.

Correlation ≠ Causation

The medication and symptom correlation features in this App identify statistical patterns in your personal data. A pattern or correlation displayed in the App does not establish a causal relationship. Any apparent association between a medication change and a symptom must be evaluated by your healthcare team before any conclusions are drawn or actions taken.

Emergency Situations

This App is not designed for emergency use. If you experience any of the following, seek emergency care immediately and do not rely on this App:

  • Sudden severe shortness of breath or respiratory distress
  • Chest pain or pressure
  • High fever with difficulty breathing
  • Sudden confusion, disorientation, or loss of consciousness
  • Any symptom you believe may be life-threatening

Call 911 or your local emergency number immediately in a medical emergency.

Post-Transplant Specific Caution

Lung transplant recipients are medically complex patients on life-sustaining immunosuppression therapy. Health changes in this population can be serious and can progress rapidly. Any concerning changes in your readings — declining lung function measurements, new or worsening symptoms, fever, unusual weight gain, or changes in lab values — should be reported to your transplant team promptly. Do not wait for this App to alert you if you feel something is wrong.

When in doubt, call your transplant team. This App is a personal tracking tool. Your transplant team is your medical safety net.

Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 2026 · Version 1.0

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data LTS Care collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

What We Collect

Required account data

  • Email address — used for account authentication and password reset only
  • Encrypted password — never stored in plain text; handled by Supabase authentication

Health tracking data (you control this)

All health measurements you log — peak flow readings, spirometer values, vital signs, lab results, medication changes, symptoms, and related notes — are stored in your personal account. This data:

  • Is stored securely in an encrypted database
  • Is accessible only to you unless you explicitly consent to share it
  • Is never sold to third parties
  • Is never shared with insurance companies, employers, or pharmaceutical companies
  • Is never used for advertising purposes

Optional profile data

If you choose to complete the optional research profile, additional information such as demographics, medical history, and lifestyle data may be collected. This is entirely voluntary. Providing or withholding this information does not affect your ability to use the App.

Technical data

  • Basic usage analytics (pages visited, features used) — anonymized
  • Device type and browser (for app compatibility purposes)
  • No precise location data is collected without explicit permission

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the App: Your health data powers your personal dashboards, charts, and trend analysis
  • To maintain your account: Email is used for authentication and critical account communications only
  • For research (only with your consent): Anonymized, de-identified data may be included in aggregate research if you have explicitly consented. Your name, email, and identifying information are never included in research datasets.
  • To improve the App: Anonymized usage patterns help us understand how to make the App more useful

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Supabase, a secure cloud database platform with:

  • Encryption at rest and in transit (TLS/SSL)
  • Row-level security — your data is accessible only to your account
  • Servers located in the United States
  • No data is stored on your device beyond what is cached for performance

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all personally identifiable data is deleted within 30 days. Anonymized, aggregated research data (if you consented) may be retained in de-identified form.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data associated with your account at any time
  • Export your health data in JSON format at any time
  • Correct any inaccurate information in your profile
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time
  • Withdraw research consent at any time from account settings
  • Opt out of any non-essential communications

Third-Party Services

LTS Care uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and storage. Subject to Supabase Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages — app hosting. Subject to GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Anthropic Claude API — used for lab result image analysis only. Images sent for analysis are not stored by Anthropic beyond the immediate API call. Subject to Anthropic Privacy Policy

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

Children's Privacy

LTS Care is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

International Users

LTS Care is operated from the United States. If you access the App from outside the United States, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

Research Data Use Policy
Last updated: June 2026 · Version 1.0

LTS Care offers users the opportunity to voluntarily contribute anonymized health data to support lung transplant research. This page explains exactly how that works.

This is Entirely Optional

Contributing data to research is completely voluntary. You can use every feature of LTS Care without contributing any research data. Your care, access, and experience in the App are identical whether you consent or not. You can change your research consent preference at any time in your account settings.

What "Anonymized" Means

Before any data is included in a research dataset, the following identifying information is permanently removed:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Exact address (city, state, and ZIP code may be retained for geographic analysis)
  • Any free-text notes that might contain identifying information
  • Transplant center name (may be retained as a general category)
  • Physician names

What remains is your health measurements, demographic categories, clinical history categories, and outcomes data — with no way to trace it back to you individually.

What Research Data May Be Used For

Anonymized data contributed through LTS Care may be used to:

  • Identify patterns in post-transplant lung function decline and recovery
  • Understand correlations between medication changes and patient-reported outcomes
  • Analyze the impact of comorbidities, geographic factors, and lifestyle on long-term transplant survival
  • Support academic publications, conference presentations, and grant applications focused on improving lung transplant outcomes
  • Inform clinical practice guidelines for post-transplant monitoring

What It Will Never Be Used For

  • It will never be sold to any commercial entity
  • It will never be shared with insurance companies, pharmaceutical companies, employers, or advertisers
  • It will never be used to make decisions about your individual care
  • It will never be used to identify you individually
  • It will never be shared with any party in a form that could identify you

Institutional Review

Any use of aggregated LTS Care data in formal academic research will be subject to appropriate Institutional Review Board (IRB) oversight as required. The voluntary, anonymized, and wellness-focused nature of data collection does not replace IRB review for formal research applications.

Your Rights as a Research Participant

  • Withdraw at any time: You can withdraw research consent from your account settings at any time. Data collected prior to withdrawal may remain in existing anonymized datasets but no new data will be included.
  • Access your contribution: You can export all your personal data at any time.
  • Ask questions: You can contact us at any time with questions about how your data is being used.
  • Receive findings: If you opt in, we will send you summaries of significant research findings derived from this dataset.

Not a Clinical Trial

Voluntary data contribution through LTS Care is not a clinical trial, observational study, or formal research protocol. It is a community wellness initiative. Participants are not research subjects in the formal sense, and no experimental interventions are involved. You are simply a person tracking your own health who has chosen to share that anonymized data for the benefit of the broader lung transplant community.

Every data point contributed helps build a clearer picture of what long-term lung transplant survival looks like — and brings the community one step closer to better outcomes for future patients.
