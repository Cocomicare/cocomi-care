Not Medical Advice

LTS Care is a personal wellness tracking application. Information, alerts, trends, and outputs do not constitute medical advice. Always seek the advice of your transplant team with any questions regarding your medical condition.

Not a Diagnostic Tool

This app does not diagnose any medical condition. Alerts and indicators are for personal awareness only — not clinical diagnoses. An alert does not mean you have a medical problem, and no alert does not mean you are medically clear.

Accuracy of Data

All data is entered by you. The app cannot verify accuracy of entries or calibration of home devices. Trends may be misleading if data is entered inconsistently. This is not a substitute for laboratory testing or clinical examination.

Medications

Never adjust, start, stop, or change any medication — especially immunosuppressants — without consulting your healthcare provider first.

Lab Results

Lab values are for personal tracking only. Clinical interpretation requires a qualified healthcare provider. Do not make medical decisions based solely on values shown here.

Correlation ≠ Causation

Patterns displayed are statistical observations only. They do not establish causal relationships and must be evaluated by your healthcare team before any action is taken.

Post-Transplant Caution

Lung transplant recipients are medically complex. Health changes can progress rapidly. Any concerning changes should be reported to your transplant team promptly. Do not wait for this app to alert you if you feel something is wrong.

No Emergency Services

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 immediately. Do not rely on this app in an emergency.

Hold Harmless

By accepting, you agree to hold harmless the creators and contributors of LTS Care from any claims, liability, or damages arising from your use of the app, including any health outcomes or medical decisions.
